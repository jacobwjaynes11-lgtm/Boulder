How the climb works

The game runs on the coin’s live trades. Nothing is scripted; the mountain only moves when the market does.

Buys push

Every buy pushes the boulder uphill. His height is the coin’s live market cap, so the bigger the pump, the further he climbs.

Sells slide

Every sell lets the boulder grind back down while he digs in his heels. Paper hands have consequences.

Pumps set him on fire

Any 3% upswing lights his feet: the price jumping 3% in a minute, a single buy that moves it 3%, or trading volume climbing 3% on the minute before. Burns light him up too.

Holders earn BTC

3% of every trade is paid to holders with $20 or more, in Bitcoin, several times an hour. Hold more, earn more.

Big buys hit different

A 1 SOL buy triggers a mighty heave. 5 SOL gives him super strength, 10 SOL sends him flying, and 15 SOL smashes him straight through the boulder.

Milestones go off

Every time the market cap passes a new milestone for the first time, fireworks fill the sky and the whole site celebrates.

Hold it, earn BTC
